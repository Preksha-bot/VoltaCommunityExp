# Laura's weekly runbook

Sign in with your individual staff account and open **Staff operations**. A normal week should need 10–15 minutes after stabilization; treat that as a pilot target rather than a measured result.

1. Check mode, last successful job, queue uncertainty/errors and upcoming conversations. Stale jobs and infrastructure failures go to the technical owner.
2. Preview the run/digest during the draft pilot. Review topic relevance, role wording and permission-sensitive cases. The system performs matching and scheduling; do not select every participant manually.
3. Review **Topic wording exceptions**. Accept a strong suggestion, change/merge the wording into a reviewed topic, or create a new reviewed topic. The founder's original wording remains recorded, and the accepted phrase becomes an alias for later classifications.
4. Review **Feedback & stories**. Feedback-only responses stay in aggregate/internal reporting. For a permitted story, compare the draft with the source feedback and permission, then approve, edit, regenerate or discard it. Laura's approval is final; do not ask the founder to approve a generated draft a second time. Anonymous stories cannot name a company. Named stories may use only the founder's authorized company name.
5. Compare completed conversations, feedback responses, reported usefulness, story-permitted feedback, drafts awaiting review and approved stories. Fictional outcomes in the demo are simulations.

**Pause** immediately stops automated founder outbound mail and new bookings. Sign-in, preferences, revocation and internal reconciliation continue. Existing meetings are not silently cancelled by unsubscribing from future opportunities. A flagged future meeting has a **Cancel meeting for privacy** action while paused. It records your staff authorization and cancels only that meeting; it cannot invite people or create a booking. An uncertain result remains for technical reconciliation.

**Resume drafts** returns to DRAFT_ONLY, never LIVE. Live activation requires the approved launch process. Do not edit Sheet formulas or add Meet links manually.

In the fictional demo, Preview run shows one consolidated draft per eligible founder. Simulate outbox records fake delivery, and Simulate booking exercises the same consent/quorum rules with a fake event. Select GROUP_READY, SAME_COMPANY_GUARD, NO_COMMON_SLOT or STORY_CASES to inspect the corresponding scenario. Reset restores the fixed clock and clears founder sessions; staff remains signed in. Demo controls are absent from the Google deployment.

A weekly staff summary goes to each active staff account's registered contact email. It contains operational counts and exceptions, with no private founder notes. Missing staff contacts appear as a configuration exception. Staff mail is separate from the founder digest limit.

Use **Mark draft reviewed** to record review of that exact rendered draft. It does not send email or activate outreach. Catalogue edits preserve topic IDs; changing labels or search synonyms cannot manufacture new match opportunities. `OTHER` stays outside automatic matching. Reusable meeting windows are saved with an explicit staff action.

For a story withdrawal, stop future publication immediately. Prior delivered email cannot be recalled. For offboarding, disable the individual account and confirm topic/snapshot/story suppression; ask the technical owner to verify any booked-event update.

The demo uses a deterministic structured classifier and story drafter so it can be reviewed without an external model.
